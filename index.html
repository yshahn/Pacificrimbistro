Our Story

A culinary voyage through Asia

Pacific Rim Bistro specializes in Pan-Asian cuisine — a vibrant journey spanning the frozen tundra of Mongolia to the tropics of Thailand, and the refined craft of Japanese sushi. We're proud to be Downtown Atlanta's premier sushi destination.

Our full sushi bar features the freshest sashimi and nigiri in the city, while our lively bar pours premium sake, signature cocktails, and an extensive wine list. Join us on the patio and soak in the energy of Atlanta's downtown bustle.

We accommodate groups of all sizes — from intimate date nights to large corporate events.
